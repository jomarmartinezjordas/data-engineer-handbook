# Steam Product Analysis and Experimentation

## User Journey with Steam
I’ve been using Steam for over five years now, starting as a college student looking for affordable PC games. At first, it was just a convenient way to buy and download games—better than physical media and full of sales. Over time, Steam evolved into a full-fledged gaming ecosystem.

What I love most about Steam today is how it brings together not only games but also community features like forums, reviews, and workshops. I use Steam to discover new indie games, participate in seasonal sales, and keep up with my favorite developers. The Steam Workshop has allowed me to customize my games, and the overlay feature makes it easy to chat with friends while playing.

With the addition of SteamVR and the Steam Deck, my experience has expanded even further—I now play games across PC, VR, and handheld devices seamlessly. Steam isn’t just a store anymore; it’s a central hub for all things gaming.

## Experiment 1: Personalized Game Discovery Feed

**Objective:** Improve game discovery by introducing a personalized feed based on playtime history, genres played, and user behavior.

- **Null Hypothesis:** A personalized game discovery feed does not increase user engagement or game purchases compared to the current recommendation system.  
- **Alternative Hypothesis:** A personalized game discovery feed increases both user engagement and game purchase rates compared to the current recommendation system.  

**Test Cell Allocation:** 50%-50%  

- **Control Group:** Users see the standard *“Recommended For You”* section based on basic algorithmic suggestions.  
- **Test Group:** Users see a dynamic, personalized feed that updates in real-time based on recent gameplay, wishlist activity, and community trends.  

**Leading Metrics:**
- Increase in time spent browsing the store.  
- Increase in clicks on game recommendations.  

**Lagging Metrics:**
- Increase in game purchase conversion rate.  
- Higher retention of active users over 3 months.

## Experiment 2: Integrated In-Game Social Hub

**Objective:** Test whether adding an integrated social overlay (similar to Discord or Xbox Live) directly within Steam improves multiplayer engagement and session length.

- **Null Hypothesis:** Adding an integrated social hub does not affect multiplayer session length or user interaction frequency.  
- **Alternative Hypothesis:** An integrated social hub increases multiplayer session length and overall user interaction within the platform.  

**Test Cell Allocation:** 50%-50%  

- **Control Group:** Users continue to rely on external tools (e.g., Discord) for communication during gameplay.  
- **Test Group:** Users have access to an in-game overlay with voice chat, text chat, friend activity feeds, and group creation tools.  

**Leading Metrics:**
- Increase in average session duration.  
- Increase in number of messages sent via the overlay.  

**Lagging Metrics:**
- Increase in daily active users.  
- Higher satisfaction scores in post-play surveys related to social connectivity.

## Experiment 3: Dynamic Wishlist Notifications

**Objective:** Test whether sending personalized, dynamic notifications about price drops, upcoming releases, and community buzz for wishlisted games increases wishlist-to-purchase conversion.

- **Null Hypothesis:** Dynamic wishlist notifications do not significantly impact the likelihood of purchasing wishlisted games.  
- **Alternative Hypothesis:** Dynamic wishlist notifications increase the probability that users will purchase games from their wishlist.  

**Test Cell Allocation:** 50%-50%  

- **Control Group:** Users receive generic email notifications about major sales or large price drops.  
- **Test Group:** Users receive smart, behavior-driven notifications tailored to each game on their wishlist (e.g., *“Game X is trending among players who liked Y,”* or *“Only 48 hours left at this low price”*).  

**Leading Metrics:**
- Increase in notification open rates.  
- Increase in visits to wishlist items after receiving a notification.  

**Lagging Metrics:**
- Higher wishlist-to-purchase conversion rate.  
- Increased average order value during sale periods.
